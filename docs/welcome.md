# Clearer

> **Five minutes. A clearer digital life.**

Welcome to the public documentation for Clearer.

Clearer is building a calmer approach to digital life.

We believe modern digital experiences have become increasingly noisy, overwhelming and demanding of our attention.

Instead of adding more features, more notifications and more complexity, we're building products that help people make small, confident decisions.

Our goal isn't perfection.

It's simply becoming **clearer than yesterday**.

---

## Why this documentation exists

We believe products should be built intentionally.

Rather than keeping our thinking hidden behind code, we're documenting the philosophy, principles and decisions that shape Clearer from day one.

This documentation is our operating system.

It explains not only **what** we're building, but **why** we're building it.

## Our Philosophy

We believe technology should help people feel lighter.

Not busier.

Not distracted.

Not overwhelmed.

Everything we build is guided by a simple question:

> **Does this make life feel clearer?**

If the answer is no, we keep refining.

---

## A Living Documentation

These documents are living references.

As Clearer evolves, they'll evolve with it.

Every meaningful change is versioned, reviewed and published publicly.

Our goal is simple:

Build in the open.

Think carefully.

Ship intentionally.
